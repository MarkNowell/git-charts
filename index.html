Chart Controls & Shortcuts

Hover Move your cursor over points, lines, or bars to reveal exact underlying data values.
Zoom & Pan Click and drag on the plot area to box-zoom. Double-click anywhere to reset the default axes.
Toggle Legend Single-click a legend item to hide/show it. Double-click an item to isolate only that series.
Toolbar Icons Hover over the top-right corner of any chart to download an image (PNG), toggle hover modes, or pan.
